\chapter{Evaluation: Dynamic Structural Graphs}
\label{sec:eval:dynamic:chapter}

We evaluate \project{} on dynamic structural graph workloads: insertion throughput scaling, mixed insert-delete workloads, and post-ingestion analytics.
We compare against Teseo, LiveGraph, and GraphOne using the GFE benchmarking driver.
The systems to which we compare are described in \autoref{sec:related-work}.
