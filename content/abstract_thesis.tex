\chapter{Abstract}

(Outline)\\
Graph-structured data is common, and there are many data domains that find natural expressions in graphs.
There is a large amount of graph-structured data being produced, and the traditional data management techniques do not directly work on graphs.
Most approaches have focused on analyzing graph data, but far fewer attempts have been made to store the graphs in easy-to-manage and easy-to-analyze formats.
We address this fundamental dichotomy in the research landscape and offer solutions to store and manage graph-structured data that is also friendly towards analytics.
We show that it is indeed possible to address both these requirements in a single system that is both performant and extensible.
